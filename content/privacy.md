This site is a personal publication by Rohit Gudi. This page describes what information is stored and which third parties receive requests when you visit.

## What is stored in your browser

The site stores one item in your browser's local storage: a preference key named "theme" that records whether you selected light or dark mode. This is stored locally on your device. No cookies are used.

## What third parties receive requests

When you visit this site, the following services may receive requests from your browser:

**GitHub Pages** hosts this site. GitHub receives requests for all pages, stylesheets, scripts, fonts, and images served from silent.engineer. [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

**jsDelivr CDN** delivers the Mermaid diagram library on pages containing diagrams. If you visit a page with diagrams, your browser requests JavaScript from cdn.jsdelivr.net. [jsDelivr Privacy Policy](https://www.jsdelivr.com/terms/privacy-policy-jsdelivr-net)

**GitHub Gist** embeds may appear in some posts. Where used, your browser loads content from gist.github.com. [GitHub General Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement)

**External images** embedded in posts may load from third-party sources. The post on SPIFFE/SPIRE embeds images from media.giphy.com and raw.githubusercontent.com.

## Analytics

This site does not use analytics services, tracking pixels, or advertising networks.
